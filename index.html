My Academic Learning Journey

Starting my academic journey at Botswana Accountancy College (BAC) has been an exciting and valuable experience. These resources allow us to access information, conduct research and work on our assignments more efficiently.

Unlike before, I have had to become more intentional about how I organise my time and approach my studies. To manage this, I use timetables and prioritise important tasks, especially when I have several academic responsibilities to complete. This helps me stay organised and make better use of my time.

Another important change I have noticed in myself is becoming more responsible and independent. I have learned to take ownership of my academic responsibilities and understand the importance of completing tasks without always depending on reminders from others. I believe this is an important part of personal growth because success in college requires commitment, discipline and the willingness to take responsibility for one's own progress.

My experience so far has also strengthened my interest in learning and developing new skills. As a Business Intelligence and Data Analytics student, I look forward to gaining more knowledge and practical experience, particularly in technology, coding and business management. I understand that learning is a continuous process, and there is always room for improvement.

If I were to give advice to someone joining college, I would encourage them to give their best in everything they do. I believe that the effort and commitment you put into your education determine how much you gain from the experience. In simple terms, if you give it your 100%, you will get as much in return.

Overall, my journey at BAC has taught me the importance of time management, responsibility, independence and continuous learning. I look forward to building on these lessons throughout my academic journey and future career.

REFLECTION 02 · TEAMWORK

My Group Work Experience

Working in a group was a valuable learning experience because it helped me understand the importance of teamwork, communication and having clearly defined responsibilities.

At the beginning, our group experienced some challenges because there were no clear roles. Nobody really knew what they had been assigned to do, which resulted in some members doing most of the work while others became less involved. This created confusion and disagreements within the group. This reflects the Forming and Storming stages of Tuckman's model, where members are still adjusting to one another and may experience difficulties working together.

Things started improving when our group leader took the initiative to assign roles and responsibilities. Members began understanding what was expected of them, and we gradually learned the importance of listening to one another. This represented the Norming stage, as we became more organised and started working together more effectively.

Eventually, everyone played their part and contributed to completing the assignment. Although we experienced difficulties at first, we managed to work together and complete the work. This demonstrated the Performing stage, where members cooperate towards achieving a common goal.

Finally, we completed and submitted the assignment before the deadline, marking the Adjourning stage of our group experience.

One of the biggest lessons I took from this experience is that clear roles and responsibilities are a core pillar of effective teamwork. I also learned that listening to others, communicating and understanding different perspectives can help resolve disagreements. Overall, this experience taught me that successful teamwork requires commitment and participation from every member.
