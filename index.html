REFLECTION 02 · TEAMWORK

My Group Work Experience

Working in a group this semester helped me understand the importance of teamwork, communication and clearly defined responsibilities. Our experience can be explained through the five stages of Tuckman's model of group development.

Forming: At the beginning, we came together as a group, but there were no clear roles or assigned responsibilities. Members were not sure what they were expected to do, so the group lacked direction.

Storming: As the work continued, the lack of clear responsibilities created challenges. Some members ended up doing most of the work while others became less involved. This led to confusion and disagreements because participation was not balanced.

Norming: The situation began to improve when our group leader took the initiative to assign roles and responsibilities. Once members understood what they were expected to do, we became more organised. We also learned that we needed to listen to one another, which helped us work through the earlier disagreements.

Performing: After becoming more organised, everyone eventually played their part and contributed to completing the assignment. The group was able to focus on the shared task instead of the confusion we experienced at the beginning.

Adjourning: We completed and submitted the assignment before the deadline. This brought our group work to a close.

The main lesson I learned is that clear roles and responsibilities are a core pillar of effective teamwork. I also learned that listening, communication and participation from every member are important when working towards a common goal. Although our group did not start smoothly, we were able to improve the way we worked together and complete the task.
